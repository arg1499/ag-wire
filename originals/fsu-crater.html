'Extremely poor performance.' When the coach supplies the obituary quotes, you know where this is going.

October 10, 2026 · 9:15 AM ET

'Extremely poor performance.' When the coach supplies the obituary quotes, you know where this is going.

"Extremely poor performance." Those were Mike Norvell's words after Florida State authored another one — and when the head coach is the one supplying the pull quotes for the program's obituary, the buyout conversation has already started in every booster chat in Tallahassee.

Owning the blame is admirable. It's also what coaches do right before they run out of time. Norvell isn't wrong about the product — FSU isn't losing close games on bad breaks. They're getting run. There's a difference between a slump and a crater, and craters don't get fixed with accountability press conferences.

The portal era was supposed to make rebuilds instant: plug holes, reload, contend. What nobody advertised is that it also makes collapses instant. Talent walks out as fast as it walks in, and when the losing starts, the exits multiply. FSU isn't rebuilding. It's free-falling, and every week the hole gets deeper and the next coach's job gets harder.

Why it matters: The next FSU coach inherits either a sleeping giant or a structural disaster, and right now it looks like the latter. In the portal era, programs don't get grace periods — they get transfer requests. The clock isn't ticking on Norvell's tenure. It's already run out; they're just negotiating the announcement.
